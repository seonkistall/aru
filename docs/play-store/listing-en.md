# ARU Google Play main store listing (en-US)

App name: `ARU`

## Short description
Narrow down K-beauty picks and routines with an optional camera check and survey

## Full description
ARU helps you narrow down K-beauty options around your preferences, budget, and everyday routine without creating an account.

If you choose the camera path, ARU first reads visible skin signals on your device. Camera use is optional. You can always complete the experience with the short survey alone when you prefer not to use a camera or when a camera is unavailable. Before capture, the app checks alignment, lighting, glare, and sharpness and explains when another photo would be more useful.

The survey asks about your skin concerns, texture preferences, ingredients you want to avoid, and budget. ARU then organizes up to three cosmetic options with clear reasons, highlighted ingredients, usage order, and points to verify. Recent results and a product start record stay on this device by default so you can return to your routine and check in later.

The default scan does not save the original full photo. An optional enhanced AI check sends a face-area crop only after separate consent. That choice is independent from research storage consent. The privacy screen lets you review and delete ARU data stored on the device.

ARU provides reference information for cosmetic selection. Check the seller for current price, stock, full ingredient details, and terms before purchasing. For pain, persistent inflammation, or sudden changes, consider speaking with a qualified health professional before choosing products.
